📌 Regional Weekend Characteristics:
• Saturday — the only guaranteed common day off for all four countries (RU, AE, SA, KZ).
• Friday — an official day off only in Saudi Arabia (SA). In Russia and Kazakhstan it is a standard working day, and in the UAE (AE) it is a shortened working day (until noon).
• Sunday — an official day off in Russia (RU), UAE (AE), and Kazakhstan (KZ). In Saudi Arabia, it is a standard working day.
